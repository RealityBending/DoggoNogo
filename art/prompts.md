# DoggoNogo asset prompts

Generated by `prompts/make_prompts.py`. Edit the asset briefs or shared style paragraph there, then re-run.

Work top to bottom: this is a pipeline, and later steps reuse what earlier ones produced. Prompts are text-only unless an **Attach as reference** line names files to attach first (paths are relative to `game/assets/`).
